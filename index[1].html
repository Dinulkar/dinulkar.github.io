01 / ABOUT

Engineer the model. Validate the reality.

My background combines simulation-led controls work with hands-on automotive testing — useful when a system needs to work both on the screen and on the vehicle.

I am an Automotive Engineer with a Master's degree in Automotive Mechatronics and Management from FH Wels, Austria. My experience spans vehicle testing, system validation, control engineering, mathematical modelling and engineering data analysis.

At BRP-Rotax I worked on nonlinear modelling, linearization and control strategies using MATLAB/Simulink. At Continental I supported sensor-data evaluation with ROS Toolbox and Foxglove. Earlier at Royal Enfield, I worked directly in R&D vehicle testing and ABS/braking validation with Tier-1 suppliers.

The common thread: understand the system, model what matters, measure the response, and improve the engineering decision.
