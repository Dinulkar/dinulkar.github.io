01// profile

About me.

I'm an automotive engineer working at the intersection of simulation, software and real hardware. My Master's at FH Wels (Automotive Mechatronics & Management) took me from transmission control theory to a thesis at BRP-Rotax — building nonlinear hydrofoil models and proving PID and MPC strategies on next-generation JetSki platforms with MATLAB/Simulink.

Before that, I spent one and a half years in Royal Enfield's R&D proving grounds, running development and type-approval tests for ABS and braking systems with Tier-1 partners like Bosch, Continental, Brembo and Showa — across four regulatory markets. At Continental, I built radar sensor evaluation pipelines with the ROS Toolbox and Foxglove for autonomous mobility programs.

What I enjoy most is the full loop: derive the model, tune the controller, validate it on the vehicle — and have the data to prove it worked.
